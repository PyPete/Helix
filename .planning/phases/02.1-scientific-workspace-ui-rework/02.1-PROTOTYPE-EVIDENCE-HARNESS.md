# UX-01 Prototype Evidence Harness

**Harness classification:** `NON_PRIMARY_PROTOTYPE`

This is a bounded Phase 3 recommendation evidence tracer, not a desktop route, layout registration, default mount, workspace implementation, or promotion decision. It is independently authored clean-room evidence and contains no IvoryOS expression, asset, terminology, layout, source, or API.

## Authority and future gate fence

The local Helix Agent is the only authority for drivers and physical execution. This harness cannot dispatch a command, grant approval, confirm execution, or perform capability, safety, lease, resource, budget, or termination validation. Every entry is proposal/read-model evidence only.

Phase 3 retains the future final gate: comparative task UAT, keyboard-only and screen-reader validation, recorded evidence, and explicit scientist approval. That gate is not executed or decided here.

## Invariant retention

- A dirty draft must use **Save**, **Discard**, or **Keep editing** before a revision, route, or procedure change. Keep editing preserves the current draft context; no draft is silently saved, retargeted, rerouted, reassigned, or published.
- Published and approved records retain exact identities, frozen references, independent procedure/plan revisions, samples, custody, and lineage.
- Phase 3 receives one original workflow-board direction. Cards and Graph are not alternate primary modes. This harness makes no primary-workspace change.
