## Binding direction (D-01 through D-11)

- **D-01:** Replace cards and graph workspace modes with one original workflow board as the primary scientific-workspace representation.
- **D-02:** Use only high-level behavioral inspiration; author the board independently.
- **D-03:** Maintain an MIT-only clean room. Do not copy or adapt IvoryOS source, UI, styles, text, layout, assets, APIs, names, logos, or branding.
- **D-04:** This direction supersedes Phase 7's prior cards-first/on-demand graph decision.
- **D-05:** Preserve **Agent-only** physical-execution authority. The board is a proposal/read-model surface and cannot address drivers, dispatch commands, approve, or confirm execution.
- **D-06:** Make the board read-only for held, reconciling, faulted, or uncertain Agent state across pointer, keyboard, shortcut, inspector, and route affordances.
- **D-07:** Provide a persistent reconciliation strip with truthful evidence and recovery navigation only; it must not resemble an execution, acknowledgement, or confirmation control.
- **D-08:** Before a pending draft changes revision, route, or procedure, require Save, Discard, or Keep editing; Keep editing retains the original draft context.
- **D-09:** Preserve **immutable** published and approved revisions, exact identities, frozen references, independent procedure/plan revisions, sample identities, custody, and lineage.
- **D-10:** Require an accessibility and comparative-UAT approval gate before downstream acceptance.
- **D-11:** Require task-based legacy comparison, keyboard-only validation, screen-reader validation, recorded outcomes/evidence, and future explicit scientist approval; no prototype, preference, or automated check can promote the direction.

## Non-negotiable scientific record constraints

Final implementation must retain immutable identities and revision/frozen references rather than replacing them with UI-local labels. It must preserve custody and lineage provenance, including append-only history and transformation facts, and it must support the six contract task types: setup, experiment, plan, run, results, and samples-analysis. The UI must truthfully expose unavailable evidence and must never infer an analytical result, sample collection, or physical execution from a planned step.

## Unresolved inputs for Phase 7

- An independently authored final board implementation and its associated clean-room provenance update.
- A decision-backed primary-workspace integration plan that preserves the legacy comparator until the UAT gate completes.
- Installed-Tauri test records containing actual Agent-backed identities and evidence locations for every UAT row.
- Documented keyboard-only and screen-reader session evidence for each representative task.
- A future scientist decision after reviewing complete recorded outcomes and evidence.

## Phase 7 acceptance prerequisites

1. Implement an independently authored final board; no prototype harness may be treated as the implementation.
2. Complete any primary-workspace change under Phase 7 ownership while preserving the authority, safety, revision, and provenance constraints above.
3. Execute full installed-Tauri task-based comparative UAT against the legacy workspace for all six views.
4. Capture keyboard-only and screen-reader evidence, observations, and evidence locations for every UAT row.
5. Obtain and record a future explicit scientist approval.

Until all prerequisites are satisfied, no final promotion is available. This recommendation assigns the work to Phase 7 and does not authorize final implementation or promotion in Phase 2.1.
